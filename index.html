Hormone and growth care for children, in Latur

Short height, early or late puberty, thyroid problems, diabetes, rickets: hormone problems in children are common and very treatable when they are found early. We measure carefully, explain clearly, and follow your child as they grow.

How we follow your child's growth

  • Height and weight plotted on Indian (IAP 2015) and WHO growth charts at every visit
  • Your child's expected height worked out from both parents' heights
  • Growth speed tracked between visits, so slowing is noticed early
  • Bone age and blood tests when needed, explained in plain words
