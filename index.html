Caring for children across Marathwada

Families come to us from Latur and the towns around it when a child is not growing as expected, when puberty comes too early or too late, or when a blood test shows a hormone problem.

Most of these problems are common, and very treatable when they are found early.

A visit built around your child

We take time to measure properly and to explain what the numbers mean, in plain words.

  1. Measure. Height, weight and body proportions, taken carefully.
  2. Chart. Plotted on Indian (IAP 2015) and WHO growth charts, next to the height expected from both parents.
  3. Explain. What is normal, what needs tests, and what the plan is.
  4. Follow up. Growth speed checked at every visit, so changes are noticed early.
