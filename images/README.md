# Photos for the website

Put photos in this folder with exactly these names. Each one appears on the site automatically.
Until a photo is added, the site shows an illustrated panel in its place, so nothing looks broken.

To see which slot is which, open the site with `#photos` at the end of the address,
e.g. https://arnav-ifkyk.github.io/pedsendoclinic/#photos

| File name | Where it appears | What to photograph | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| hero.jpg | Top of the page, full width | Doctor measuring a child's height, or the clinic entrance | Wide (landscape), at least 2000 px wide |
| stack-1.jpg | Front card of the photo stack | Latur Diabetes Centre / clinic entrance | Square |
| stack-2.jpg | Middle card | Consulting room | Square |
| stack-3.jpg | Back card | Waiting area | Square |
| measure-1.jpg | "Height and growth charts" card | Growth chart on the desk, or the height measuring board | 4:3 |

| measure-4.jpg | "Blood tests" card | Lab tubes / sample collection area | 4:3 |
| follow-1.jpg | Full-screen panel 1 | Doctor reviewing a growth chart | Wide, at least 2000 px |

Tips
- Use JPG, compressed to around 300–500 KB each (squoosh.app is free and easy).
- **Get written consent from parents before using any photo of a child or patient.** Avoid showing faces if unsure.
- No patient names, reports or screens with patient data visible in any photo.
